\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

This section reports the compact public-data execution of FireSmokeGenNet.
It replaces the previous manuscript Results tables. All numerical values
are taken from the JSON measurements written by the compact pipeline.
Methods that were not run---Stable Diffusion, PowerPaint, BLD,
FlameDiffuser, official YOLO v6--v13 at \(640\times 640\), and registered
FLAME / HPWREN / SMOKE5K domain splits---are omitted rather than copied
from an earlier draft.

Images were \(64\times 64\) with a stride-4 TinyVAE (latent \(16\times 16\)).
The U-Net used widths 64--192, a cosine schedule with \(T=100\),
classifier-free guidance, and 8-step DDIM. The generator was trained for
160 iterations with MRDL \(\omega=0.4\). Quality ranking used the paper
weights \(Q=0.4\,\mathrm{color}+0.4\,\mathrm{visibility}+0.2\,\mathrm{translucency}\)
and retained the top 30\% of candidates. Source-level 80/10/10 splits used
camera identifiers as the source key, with SHA-256 exact-duplicate removal
and perceptual-hash near-duplicate control. Downstream inference used five
matched seeds \(\{42,123,3407,2025,2026\}\). Public substitutes were
Pyro-SDIS smoke frames and Wikimedia landscape backgrounds. Masks were
obtained by GrabCut inside the YOLO box followed by the largest connected
component. The host was CPU-only. This is a methodology-faithful run, not
a bit-identical replica of the \(512\)\,px / 20k-step / Qwen2-VL-7B /
YOLO@640 protocol.

\subsection{Vision--Language Quality-Predictor Validation}
\label{sec:results-vlm}

The original protocol specifies a LoRA-tuned Qwen2-VL-7B ranker on 150
human-rated images. That 7B teacher does not fit in the present CPU memory
budget. The same three axes, the same composite weights, the same top-30\%
keep rule, and the same five-fold student-versus-teacher protocol were
retained. The teacher was a heuristic scorer; the student was a small MLP
on image features.

Table~\ref{tab:vlm} reports mean \(\pm\) sample standard deviation across
the five folds. The student follows the teacher most closely on visibility
(MAE \(0.97\) on a 0--10 scale) and least closely on color (MAE \(2.71\)).
Composite rank correlation is weak and high-variance
(\(\rho=0.11\pm 0.25\); fold-wise
\(\rho\in\{0.45,-0.25,0.06,0.20,0.06\}\)).
These measurements support the pipeline structure---score three axes, form
\(Q\), keep the top 30\%---but do not reproduce the original Qwen2-VL-7B
agreement (\(\rho=0.83\)). The ranker is treated only as a within-study
filter on this 96-candidate pool. Fig.~\ref{fig:vlm} visualizes per-axis MAE.

\subsection{Background Preservation and Text--Image Alignment}
\label{sec:results-bg}

PSNR, SSIM, and the 1-LPIPS proxy were computed over the preserved region
\(1-M\) after identical 64\,px resizing and \([0,1]\) normalization.
Prompt similarity is cosine similarity between the generated RGB image and
a bag-of-words embedding of the caption (CLIP ViT-H/14 was not loaded).
Only methods that were actually sampled are reported. Alpha-blend copies
the unmasked background exactly, so its PSNR/SSIM are not a fair
inpainting comparison.

\hline
Method & PSNR \(\uparrow\) & SSIM \(\uparrow\) & 1-LPIPS \(\uparrow\) & Prompt-sim \(\uparrow\) \\
\hline
Alpha-blend compositing & 60.00 & 1.00 & --- & --- \\
FireSmokeGenNet (raw decode) & 10.08 & 0.15 & 0.80 & 0.34 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The dual-branch generator, trained for 160 steps from a randomly
initialized TinyVAE, does not preserve the background at the original
paper-scale fidelity (PSNR \(28.10\)\,dB, SSIM \(0.88\)). The
\(10.08\)\,dB / \(0.15\) numbers measure this compact decoder, not smoke
realism inside the mask. They are reported because the metric definitions
match the experimental protocol; they are not a claim that the 64\,px
model matches SD-2 inpainting.

\subsection{Boundary Softness Analysis}
\label{sec:results-bound}

The boundary band is
\(B_\delta(M)=\mathrm{Dilate}(M,\delta)\setminus\mathrm{Erode}(M,\delta)\)
with \(\delta\) scaled to 64\,px. Softness \(S\) is the mean gradient
magnitude in that band. KL uses 100-bin histograms of \(\|\nabla I\|_2\)
with a \(10^{-8}\) floor.

\begin{table}[!t]
\centering
\caption{Boundary-quality metrics. Real-smoke softness is a reference, not
a method.}
\label{tab:bound}
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\hline
Method & Boundary softness \(S\) & KL\((\mathrm{real}\,\|\,\mathrm{synth})\) \(\downarrow\) \\
\hline
Real smoke & 0.363 & --- \\
Alpha-blend compositing & 0.438 & 15.20 \\
FireSmokeGenNet & \textbf{0.310} & 15.31 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

FireSmokeGenNet is nearer the real reference than compositing
(\(0.310\) versus \(0.363\); blend \(0.438\)), i.e., less abrupt
mask-edge gradients. KL is comparable at this capacity (\(15.31\) versus
\(15.20\)). The originally published gap (softness \(0.124\) versus real
\(0.119\); KL \(0.041\) versus \(0.187\)) is not recovered at 64\,px /
160 steps. Softness is a local gradient descriptor, not a claim of
perceptual equivalence to real plumes. Fig.~\ref{fig:bound} shows the
three softness values.

\subsection{Downstream Detection Performance}
\label{sec:results-det}

Official YOLO families at 640\,px were not executed. Pyro-SDIS median box
area is approximately \(0.17\%\) of the image; at 64--128\,px the box is
on the order of 10\,px, and IoU \(0.5\) is unstable. All eight compact
family stand-ins produced \(\mathrm{AP}_{50}=0\). Those zeros are not
filled into Table~\ref{tab:downstream}, and the previously published
YOLOv13 numbers (\(78.16\pm 0.27\%\rightarrow 80.88\pm 0.70\%\)) are not
copied.

The runnable downstream protocol is image-level smoke-versus-background
classification with a frozen ImageNet ResNet-18, five matched seeds, and a
test set of real smoke plus held-out backgrounds. Mixed training adds the
29 quality-filtered FireSmokeGenNet images to the real training set.

\subsection{Discussion}
\label{sec:results-disc}

\subsubsection{What this run supports}
By processing the mask and masked image through separate encoder
branches, dual-branch generation is closer to real Pyro-SDIS smoke than
alpha-blend compositing in Feature-FD and MMD\(^2\)
(Table~\ref{tab:dist}). Boundary softness is nearer the real reference
than compositing (Table~\ref{tab:bound}), while KL remains comparable at
this capacity. The three-axis composite and top-30\% keep rule were
executed; student--teacher \(\rho\) is too weak to claim a calibrated VLM
(Table~\ref{tab:vlm}). Mixed training raised five-seed classification
accuracy by \(+6.98\) percentage points (Table~\ref{tab:downstream}). The
paired test is not significant at \(\alpha=0.05\) on \(n=5\). This is a
directional proxy, not a YOLO \(\mathrm{AP}_{50}\) result.

\subsubsection{What this run does not support}
The compact execution does not support paper-scale background PSNR/SSIM
(\(28.10\)\,dB / \(0.88\)), YOLOv6--v13 \(\mathrm{AP}_{50}\) (including
the mixed YOLOv13 value of \(80.88\%\)), domain-shift AP on registered
wildfire-camera seasons, component / \(\omega\) / retention-threshold
rankings by AP, or Qwen2-VL-7B MAE/Spearman from the original VLM table.

\subsubsection{Limitations specific to the compact execution}
Tiny smoke boxes make IoU-0.5 detection degenerate. 160 generator steps
and a TinyVAE underfit background reconstruction. Heuristic quality
labels are not human ratings. The classification test set is small; mixed
accuracy saturates at 100\%, which compresses variance and inflates
\(d_z\) relative to a harder detector task.

\subsubsection{How to obtain the missing tables}
Run the paper-scale configuration on an A100-class GPU with official YOLO
weights, CLIP ViT-H/14, Qwen2-VL-7B, and the registered FLAME / HPWREN /
SMOKE5K sources. Until that run exists, those cells stay empty.
